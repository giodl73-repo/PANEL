## Overall Assessment

The revision makes significant progress on the human-centered dimensions I raised in Round 1. The new author experience section provides the human perspective that was entirely missing — the structured reflection on trust calibration, the craft-vs-vision distinction, and the design space for interactivity are all valuable additions. The expanded ethical framework addresses consent, attribution, and agency concerns appropriately.

The discussion of author agency — distinguishing "address" from "resolve" and noting that authors should be empowered rather than constrained — reflects genuine engagement with my concern about compliance-oriented design. The crowdsourcing literature connection is also well-handled.

I remain slightly concerned that the paper is primarily a systems/methodology paper with human-centered analysis added as a revision rather than integrated from the start. But the revisions are substantive enough that the paper now presents a balanced view. The interactivity design space discussion opens important future work directions.

## Minor Issues

### m1: Interactivity Remains Future Work
The interactive mode design space (author clarification, focus steering, dialogue) is sketched but not implemented. For a CHI audience, a prototype or wizard-of-oz study of interactive review would significantly strengthen the contribution.

### m2: Process Flow Diagram Still Missing
I suggested a visual process diagram in Round 1 (P3.2) and it hasn't been added. A well-designed diagram showing the 8-stage lifecycle with the recheck loop would improve the paper's accessibility.
