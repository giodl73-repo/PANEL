## Priority 1: Blocking Issues

Issues that must be addressed before resubmission. Raised by 3+ reviewers or flagged as major by any reviewer.

### P1.1: Evaluation Circularity — Self-Referential Metrics
**Raised by**: Bernstein, Liang, Amershi, Shankar (4/5 reviewers)
**Description**: The paper's primary evidence — score improvement from 5.6/10 to 7.4/10 — is generated by the same AI system that produces the reviews. This creates circular evaluation: the system evaluates its own effectiveness. No external validation is provided.
**Impact**: Without external grounding, all quantitative claims (32% improvement, 85% readiness) lack validity. This undermines the paper's core contribution.
**Recommended action**: Add at least one form of external validation: (a) compare AI reviews against actual venue reviews for submitted papers, (b) recruit 2-3 human experts for blind comparison on a subset, or (c) demonstrate correlation between AI scores and some external quality measure (e.g., acceptance rates).

### P1.2: Missing Baselines and Ablation Studies
**Raised by**: Bernstein, Shneiderman, Amershi, Shankar (4/5 reviewers)
**Description**: The paper does not compare the full methodology against simpler alternatives. It is unclear whether improvements come from the structured lifecycle, the AI personas, or simply receiving any LLM feedback. Key missing comparisons: (a) unstructured LLM feedback without personas, (b) structured self-review checklists, (c) generic AI feedback with the same lifecycle but no personas.
**Impact**: Cannot attribute value to the specific design choices (personas, 8-stage lifecycle, priority triage). The methodology's claimed contributions may be overfit to the structured pipeline rather than the persona framework.
**Recommended action**: Design an ablation study with at least two conditions: (1) full methodology vs. (2) same lifecycle with generic (non-persona) AI feedback. If possible, add a third condition with unstructured single-prompt feedback.

### P1.3: Missing Statistical Rigor
**Raised by**: Liang, Shankar (2/5 reviewers — but flagged as major by both)
**Description**: All reported metrics are point estimates without uncertainty quantification. Missing: confidence intervals, significance tests (paired t-test for improvement claims), effect sizes, run-to-run variance analysis, and per-module breakdowns.
**Impact**: Without statistical analysis, the reported improvements could be within noise range. The 14-paper sample is small enough that variance could dominate.
**Recommended action**: Add confidence intervals and paired significance tests for all improvement claims. Run a variance analysis by regenerating reviews multiple times for at least 3 papers and reporting score stability.

### P1.4: No Reproducibility Details for Core Pipeline
**Raised by**: Liang, Shankar (2/5 reviewers — flagged as major by both)
**Description**: The paper does not provide the prompts used for review generation, the LLM model version/parameters, token counts, or how persona information is injected into prompts. For a methodology paper, this prevents anyone from reproducing or independently validating the results.
**Impact**: Fundamentally undermines the paper's claim of providing a "reproducible framework." Without prompt templates and model specifications, the methodology is a description, not a specification.
**Recommended action**: Include complete prompt templates (with variable placeholders), model version, temperature, and sampling parameters. Ideally provide these as supplementary material.

## Priority 2: Important Improvements

Issues that would significantly strengthen the paper. Raised by 2+ reviewers.

### P2.1: Human Experience and Interactivity Gap
**Raised by**: Shneiderman, Amershi
**Description**: The paper presents a batch-mode pipeline with no human-in-the-loop interaction. There is no mechanism for author-reviewer dialogue, no author steering of reviewer focus, no study of how authors experience AI-generated reviews. For CHI/CSCW, this gap in interactivity and human experience data is significant.
**Recommended action**: (a) Add at minimum an author experience case study with structured reflection. (b) Discuss the design space for interactive modes (author clarification before synthesis, steering reviewer focus). (c) Consider adding a mechanism for author pushback on contested P1 items.

### P2.2: Ethical and Agency Concerns
**Raised by**: Bernstein, Shneiderman
**Description**: The paper uses real researchers' names for persona construction with minimal ethical discussion. Putting specific feedback in named researchers' mouths raises attribution and consent concerns. Additionally, the gate-based lifecycle reduces author agency to compliance (addressing P1 items) without space for creative disagreement.
**Recommended action**: Expand Section 6.3 into a full ethical framework addressing: consent for name use, risk of misrepresentation, author agency in the review process, and the distinction between simulated and actual expert opinion.

### P2.3: Missing Process and Operational Metrics
**Raised by**: Bernstein, Shankar
**Description**: The paper reports outcome metrics (score improvement) but not process metrics: P1/P2/P3 item counts per paper, average revision effort, loop frequency, cost per paper (time, tokens, API calls), latency per stage, failure rates.
**Recommended action**: Add a process metrics table: average P1/P2/P3 counts, rounds-to-completion distribution, cost per paper, time per stage.
