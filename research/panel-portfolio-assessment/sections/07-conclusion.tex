\section{Conclusion}

Cross-portfolio expert panels provide a structured methodology for assessing multi-paper research programs holistically. The approach produces tier-classified rankings, identifies cross-cutting themes invisible at the individual paper level, and generates strategic recommendations through structured reviewer disagreement.

Applied to a 13-paper program across two modules, the methodology identified 6 cross-cutting themes, produced a coherent submission strategy, and demonstrated that portfolio-level review surfaces qualitatively different insights from individual paper assessment. The structured disagreement between reviewer blocs provides richer information than simple score aggregation.

The methodology is applicable to any multi-paper research effort---from dissertation committees assessing a candidate's body of work to research groups evaluating their collective output before submission season.

Future work includes: (1) validation across diverse research programs, (2) comparison of AI-simulated panels against human expert panels, (3) longitudinal tracking of panel recommendations through actual submission outcomes, and (4) automated panel composition optimization.
