\section{Related Work}

\subsection{Research Program Assessment}

Research assessment at the program level has been studied in the context of national research evaluation frameworks (UK REF, Australian ERA). These operate at the departmental or institutional level. Our methodology operates at the individual researcher or small-team level, assessing coherent paper collections rather than departmental output.

\subsection{Meta-Review and Area Chairing}

Conference area chairs perform a form of portfolio assessment, reading multiple reviews and papers to make accept/reject decisions. Our methodology formalizes this role into a structured panel process with explicit ranking, tier classification, and cross-cutting theme identification.

\subsection{Bibliometric Portfolio Analysis}

Bibliometric approaches assess research programs through citation metrics, h-indices, and impact factors. These are retrospective and lag actual quality by years. Our approach is prospective: it assesses pre-submission quality and identifies improvement opportunities before publication.
