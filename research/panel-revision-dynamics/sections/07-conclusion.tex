\section{Conclusion}

We have characterized the dynamics of multi-round paper revision through AI-simulated expert review across 14 papers. The key findings---32\% average score improvement, P1 items accounting for 72\% of gains, 85\% submission readiness within two rounds, and logarithmic diminishing returns---provide empirical grounding for iterative review process design.

These findings have practical implications: budget two review rounds, prioritize P1 items aggressively, and expect diminishing returns beyond round 2. The P1/P2/P3 triage system is validated as an effective mechanism for directing revision effort.

Future work includes: (1) comparing AI-simulated revision dynamics against real venue rebuttal outcomes, (2) multi-author validation to separate revision skill from process effectiveness, (3) automated revision strategy recommendation based on initial review patterns, and (4) longitudinal tracking of papers through actual venue submission and review.
