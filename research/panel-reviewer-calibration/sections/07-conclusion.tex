\section{Conclusion}

We have demonstrated that AI reviewer personas can be calibrated to produce measurably distinct, expertise-consistent feedback through profile-based prompting without fine-tuning. The key calibration mechanisms---key-question injection, expertise categorization, and venue alignment---produce reviewer panels with genuine multi-perspective assessment capability, as evidenced by bloc formation, low between-bloc correlations, and high topic focus divergence.

The method is practical: constructing a new persona requires only a structured profile (name, affiliation, expertise, key question, venue alignment), and panels can be assembled in minutes. The 45+ persona database provides coverage across major AI/ML research areas, and the framework is extensible to new domains.

Future work includes: (1) cross-model calibration validation, (2) automated persona optimization (learning which profile fields most affect distinctness), (3) calibration against real venue reviews when submission outcomes become available, and (4) persona databases for additional research domains beyond AI/ML.
